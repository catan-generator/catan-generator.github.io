What is this Catan board generator?

This free online tool creates balanced, fair random Catan boards for the base game (3–4 players). Unlike simply shuffling tiles, it uses a constraint-solving algorithm so that every generated map is guaranteed to follow the placement rules you choose — the red numbers 6 & 8 are never adjacent, 2 & 12 stay apart, identical numbers don't cluster, and the same resources are spread across the island. Every board has a seed: share it with friends and they can open the exact same map. The app is free, has no ads, and works offline as a PWA.

Frequently asked questions

Why should 6 and 8 not touch in Catan?
6 and 8 are rolled most often. When two red numbers sit on adjacent hexes, one settlement spot becomes overwhelmingly strong and the game feels decided at setup. Keeping them apart is the most common house rule for a fair start.
What makes a Catan board "balanced"?
A balanced board spreads high-probability numbers and resource types evenly, so no starting corner dominates. This generator enforces that with hard rules instead of luck.
Can I replay the same map?
Yes — every map is generated from a seed shown in the toolbar. Enter the same seed with the same rules and you get an identical board on any device.
