Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026 · Applies to the Hex Board mobile app and this website.

Website (catan-generator.github.io)

The board generator on this site runs entirely in your browser. We set no cookies, run no analytics and collect no personal data. Generated maps and seeds never leave your device.

Hex Board app — what we collect

  • Account data (optional). The generator and a local score board work without an account. If you sign in (email or Google), your display name and email are stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Guests get an anonymous session — a random identifier, no personal data.
  • Score board data. Player names, match dates and scores you enter. Local boards stay on your device; shared boards live in Cloud Firestore and are visible only to people who joined with the board's invite code.
  • Usage & stability. Firebase Analytics (anonymous events) and Crashlytics (crash reports). No advertising ID; the app contains no ads.

What we do NOT do

  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No contacts, location, photos, microphone or file access.
  • No ads, no advertising SDKs.

Storage & security

Data lives on your device (encrypted storage) and in Google Firebase, protected by security rules: only members of a shared board can read or write its data.

Your choices & deletion

Use the app without an account, delete boards you own in the app, or email us to delete your account and profile data entirely.

Children

Hex Board is a utility for board-game players and is not directed at children under 13.
